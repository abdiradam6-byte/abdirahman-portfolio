About me

A commitment to evidence, equity, and better health outcomes.

I am a Public Health and Tropical Medicine undergraduate at Somali National University with a growing focus on epidemiology, research methods, health data, and community health. I am motivated by the role reliable local evidence can play in identifying public health priorities and improving the design and delivery of services.

My interests include maternal and child health, immunization, nutrition, WASH, digital health, infectious disease prevention, and health systems strengthening in Somalia. I value collaborative research, ethical data practices, and communicating findings clearly to support practical action.
